Welcome, Animal Intelligence Detective!

Your mission: investigate whether animals truly think, act on instinct, or learn trained behaviors. Unlock four case files by reading short mini-lessons and analyzing evidence.

How it works: Complete 12 multiple-choice challenges. Each question has one best answer. Work independently; use the optional read-aloud button. Your answers save on this device. At the end, submit your case file to receive a score out of 25 points, then take a screenshot to upload to Canvas.

This game is based on the supplied Part 1 follow-along notes for Dorothy Hinshaw Patent's “How Smart Are Animals?” It is a companion, not a replacement for the full reading/listening selection.
